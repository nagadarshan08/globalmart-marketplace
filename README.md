# GlobalMart 🌎

GlobalMart is a modern marketplace platform designed to connect local businesses, vendors, resellers, and buyers through one trusted digital marketplace.

The platform focuses on helping local businesses showcase their products to a wider customer base while providing buyers with a simple and transparent shopping experience.

---

## 🚀 Project Overview

GlobalMart is designed around a multi-user marketplace ecosystem.

The main users of the platform are:

- **Vendors** – List and manage products and reach more customers.
- **Buyers** – Discover products, place orders, and track deliveries.
- **Resellers** – Source products from vendors and resell them to customers.
- **Admins** – Manage users, vendors, products, and marketplace activity.

The current project focuses on the **frontend landing page and UI/UX prototype** for GlobalMart.

---

The GlobalMart landing page includes:
